# **App Name**: Story Weaver AI

## Core Features:

- Story Seed Input: Allows the user to input a brief (1-2 sentence) story seed to initiate novel generation, along with defining the maximum word count (up to 100,000 words).
- Advanced Customization Settings: A comprehensive panel for users to define novel genre, tone, character details, world-building elements, and other specific creative constraints for the AI.
- AI Plot Outlining Tool: Utilizes AI to construct a precise, logical, and chronologically accurate story outline, dividing the novel into parts, main events, and chapter-by-chapter summaries, ensuring no repetition or irrelevant content.
- AI Chapter Generation Tool: An AI tool that writes the novel chapter by chapter, strictly adhering to the generated outline, user's customization settings, and maintaining narrative coherence without repetition or filler.
- Real-time Generation Progress: A dynamic display showing the novel's generation status, including current chapter being written, overall word count progress, and other relevant generation statistics.
- Instant Chapter Display: Automatically renders each generated chapter in a readable format as it's completed, allowing users to review the evolving narrative in real-time.

## Style Guidelines:

- A dark color scheme that promotes focus and reduces eye strain during extended reading and writing sessions. The primary interactive color is a deep, creative purple (#6347EB), evoking imagination and depth. The background is a very dark, muted shade of the same purple (#211F2E) for subtlety and visual comfort. An accent color, a soft, lighter blue-purple (#9FB5DF), is used to highlight key information and provide gentle visual contrast.
- Headline font: 'Inter' (grotesque sans-serif) for its modern, clean, and objective appearance in UI elements and titles. Body text font: 'Alegreya' (humanist serif) for its elegant, intellectual, and contemporary feel, ideal for long-form narrative readability.
- Utilize minimalist, sophisticated line icons related to writing, progress, settings, and narrative elements to maintain a clean and focused interface.
- A structured and organized layout, possibly featuring a two-pane design, separating customization and progress controls from the main novel content. Emphasize ample white space and clear visual hierarchy to enhance readability and user interaction, allowing the story to be the central focus.
- Implement subtle and elegant animations to provide feedback on generation progress, chapter completion, and interaction states, ensuring a smooth and responsive user experience without distraction.
